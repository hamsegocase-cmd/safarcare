Clear about what we do, and what we don't

Travel information has to be right. So SafarCare is built to be honest about its limits.

  • We never guess

    If a detail isn't on your ticket, you'll see “Not found” and what to ask your airline.

  • We don't decide visas

    Visa rules depend on your passport and change often. SafarCare reminds you to check with the embassy or immigration authority instead.

  • Your ticket stays on your phone

    Tickets are read on your device and saved encrypted. An account is optional and only backs up your trip details — never the ticket file.

  • No ads, no selling data

    Your documents are never shown to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. Delete anything at any time.

Questions

Is SafarCare free?

Yes. SafarCare is free and has no advertisements.

Which tickets can it read?

E-ticket screenshots, photos of printed tickets, PDF tickets and booking confirmations, and ticket images sent on WhatsApp. If a picture is too blurry, the app tells you how to take a better one.

Can it tell me if I need a visa?

No. It reminds you to check visa, transit and passport requirements with the official authority, and lets you keep notes on what they told you.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account only backs up your trips so you can get them back on a new phone.
